AI Studios video-highlight and video-series features

Two AI Studios 4.0 features for content creators. Video Highlight finds and cuts the best moments of a video; Video Series generates videos on a schedule.

Problem

Content creators repeat the same production steps for every video. AI Studios 4.0 added features that automate them from a user’s keywords, videos, or files: periodic video generation and automatic highlight extraction, built on a multimodal AI pipeline.

What I built

Video Highlight

  • Finding highlights from speech. The Azure Speech SDK converts a video’s audio into a timestamped script. The script goes to the automation server, where an LLM picks out the highlight segments.
  • Post-processing and rendering. The video is cut to those segments and captions are added; the result is sent to the rendering server to produce the final video.
  • Fallbacks. When there is no script, YouTube comment data feeds the LLM analysis instead. The Google Video Intelligence API adds multimodal signals such as scene changes and person detection.

Video Series

  • Input UI. Users enter keywords, video settings, or a spreadsheet to register generation tasks.
  • Scheduler. A Kubernetes CronJob runs every 10 minutes, queries the database for pending tasks, and requests video generation. I handled MongoDB transactions and cluster management.
  • Error handling. Each task ends as success, error, or timeout. Failed tasks are retried up to three times, then abandoned with an error alert.

Results

  • With the fallbacks, highlight extraction kept a success rate above 90% even when no script was available.
  • Video Series fully automated recurring video generation, from the initial file or URL to the finished video.
